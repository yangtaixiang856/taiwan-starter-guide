## Important Content Direction

The site should not only answer questions vaguely. It should give concrete, useful details.

Bad style:

> Bring required documents and check with the bank.

Better style:

> Start with your employer payroll bank or your school-recommended branch. Bring passport, ARC or resident certificate, Taiwan phone number, Chinese address, student or employment proof, and extra ID if you have it. If one branch refuses, ask what document is missing and try a larger branch near a university, business district, or your workplace.

Tone:

- friendly
- practical
- trustworthy
- human
- specific
- not too formal
- not overconfident on legal, tax, visa, labor, health, or financial rules

For sensitive topics, always include:

- rules may change
- check official sources
- this is educational information, not legal, tax, financial, immigration, or labor advice

## Monetization Assumptions

First 3 months:

- Do not rely on AdSense.
- Focus on useful content, tools, and outbound click testing.
- Use low-risk affiliate categories only.

Possible low-risk affiliate categories:

- SIM / eSIM
- airport pickup or transport passes
- travel cards
- short-term accommodation
- family visit accommodation
- daily life products
- student arrival checklist products

Avoid or delay:

- remittance affiliate
- financial product promotion
- anything that may trigger Taiwan financial regulation concerns

If financial topics are mentioned:

- use official source links
- do not make the site look like a financial service provider
- include "not a financial service provider" and "information only" disclaimers
