How do foreigners file income tax in Taiwan?

Use this page to understand the workflow before you talk to eTax, a local tax office, your employer, or a tax professional.

Updated 2026-05-20 Official sources checked 2026-05-20 For students, expats, and workers Educational, not tax advice

Short answer

Gather your withholding statement, income records, passport or ARC details, deduction documents, and residency-day count for the taxable year. Then verify your filing method with eTax or the local National Taxation Bureau office before you submit anything.

What to prepare

  • Passport and ARC or residence information, if applicable.
  • Withholding statement or salary records from your employer.
  • Documents for deductions, dependents, or special situations.
  • Your approximate days in Taiwan during the taxable year.
  • A backup plan if online filing login does not work for you.

Realistic example

A foreign teacher has salary income in Taiwan but cannot log in with a card reader on a Mac. Instead of guessing, he saves his withholding statement, checks his ARC/passport details, visits the local tax office during filing season, and asks staff which filing route fits his documents.

Common mistake

Do not assume your employer's withholding equals your final tax result. Withholding, final filing, deductions, and residency treatment are related but not the same thing.

Official source check

This page was checked against the Ministry of Finance eTax alien individual income tax pages on 2026-05-20. eTax separates foreign taxpayers by length of stay in the taxable year, points readers to required documents, and lists National Taxation Bureau offices for local help.

  • Taiwan's taxable year is January 1 to December 31.
  • The 90-day and 183-day thresholds affect how foreigner tax questions are handled.
  • Resident treatment, deductions, and progressive tax rates are separate questions from basic employer withholding.
  • If your online login, card reader, or document situation does not fit the normal path, contact the tax office instead of guessing.

If online filing does not work

Some foreigners run into a very practical problem: the tax website, card reader, browser, or digital certificate route does not work for them. Prepare your documents, check the required-document page, and ask the National Taxation Bureau office which route applies to your case.

Example: A student used a Mac and could not get the reader to work. She downloaded her salary withholding records, printed her ARC and passport copies, wrote down her days in Taiwan, and brought everything to the tax office instead of trying random settings for hours.
