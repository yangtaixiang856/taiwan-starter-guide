# Taiwan Starter Guide Monetization Roadmap

Last updated: 2026-05-22

Purpose: Build a low-risk income path for Taiwan Starter Guide without making the site feel like an affiliate farm. This roadmap does not add affiliate links. It defines when and where monetization can be tested after the site proves user demand.

## Core Principle

Taiwan Starter Guide should earn trust before it tries to earn money.

For the first stage, the site should help newcomers solve real Taiwan arrival problems: phone data, transport, housing, cost planning, documents, banking, NHI, ARC, work permits, and school or employer questions. Monetization should only appear where it clearly helps the user take the next step.

Avoid anything that makes the site look like a financial service provider, immigration adviser, labor consultant, tax adviser, or medical adviser.

## First 30 Days: No Ads, No Affiliate Push

Do not place display ads or affiliate blocks during the first 30 days after public beta.

Use this period to measure:

- Which pages people actually read.
- Which guide cards or SEO pages get clicks.
- Which outbound links people use.
- Which search terms bring visitors.
- Which feedback messages repeat.
- Whether visitors use the cost tool or tax tool.
- Whether people subscribe, send feedback, or share the site.

Allowed during this period:

- Neutral official-source links.
- Non-affiliate links to useful platforms or official services.
- Beta feedback form.
- Newsletter form.
- Internal links to deeper guides and tools.

Not allowed during this period:

- AdSense.
- Affiliate banners.
- Product blocks that look sponsored.
- Financial product promotion.
- Remittance affiliate links.

## First 90 Days: Do Not Depend On AdSense

AdSense should not be the main income plan in the first 90 days.

Reasons:

- Thin or early-stage sites may look low quality to ad networks.
- Ads can make a trust-building site feel cheap too early.
- Income will likely be small until organic traffic is stable.
- The site needs content depth first, especially for sensitive topics.

During the first 90 days, the better goal is to validate:

- Which topics bring repeat traffic.
- Which pages create useful outbound clicks.
- Which pages people save or share.
- Which low-risk recommendation categories fit naturally.

## Pages Suitable For Future Affiliate Testing

These pages can support low-risk affiliate testing after they have enough traffic and the content is useful without the affiliate block.

### Best First Candidates

- `guides/sim-card-esim-taiwan.html`
  - Best category: SIM / eSIM.
  - Reason: phone data is a first-week need.
  - Condition: page should first explain airport SIM, eSIM, prepaid SIM, monthly plans, documents, and signal testing.

- `guides/easycard-transportation-taiwan.html`
  - Best category: airport pickup, transport passes, city passes.
  - Reason: transport is useful to every newcomer.
  - Condition: official MRT, bus, TRA, HSR, EasyCard, and YouBike information must stay primary.

- `guides/cost-of-living-taipei-new-taipei.html`
  - Best category: daily life products, transport, short-term accommodation.
  - Reason: users are already thinking about monthly budget.
  - Condition: estimates must stay clearly labeled as examples, not promises.

- `guides/renting-room-in-taiwan.html`
  - Best category: short-term accommodation before long-term rental, moving-in products.
  - Reason: users may need temporary housing before signing a lease.
  - Condition: do not promote unsafe rentals, unclear leases, or anything that bypasses proper housing checks.

- Future `guides/prepare-before-moving-to-taiwan.html`
  - Best category: arrival checklist products, SIM / eSIM, airport transport, temporary accommodation.
  - Reason: this is the most natural page for "what should I prepare before I land?"
  - Condition: page must be built as a practical checklist first.

- Future `guides/convenience-stores-taiwan.html`
  - Best category: daily life products.
  - Reason: low-risk daily-life context.
  - Condition: do not turn it into a product catalog.

## Pages Not Suitable For Affiliate Blocks

Do not place affiliate blocks on pages where the user is looking for official, sensitive, or high-stakes information.

Avoid affiliate blocks on:

- `guides/taiwan-tax-for-foreigners.html`
- `guides/183-day-tax-rule.html`
- `guides/arc-resident-certificate.html`
- `guides/nhi-for-foreigners.html`
- `guides/work-permit-international-students.html`
- Future job contract checklist page
- Future salary records and payslips page
- Future overtime and leave page
- Future emergency contacts page
- Future official verification page
- Any remittance, banking, loan, credit card, investment, or financial product page

Reason:

- These topics need trust and caution.
- Readers may be stressed, vulnerable, or making official decisions.
- Monetization here could damage credibility.
- Some areas may trigger legal, financial, labor, tax, immigration, or health-insurance concerns.

## Topics To Avoid For Now

Do not test affiliate offers in these categories during the first monetization phase:

- Remittance.
- Credit cards.
- Loans.
- Investment accounts.
- Insurance.
- Brokerage accounts.
- Crypto.
- Debt products.
- Financial comparison tools.
- Paid immigration services.
- Paid tax filing services.
- Paid labor dispute services.
- Anything that looks like Taiwan financial product promotion.

If these topics are covered at all, keep them informational and source-backed. Link to official agencies or neutral official resources first.

## Disclosure Wording

Every future affiliate area should have a clear disclosure near the recommendation, not hidden only in the footer.

### Short Disclosure

Use this near small recommendation blocks:

> Disclosure: Some future links on this page may be partner links. If you buy through them, Taiwan Starter Guide may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only include options that fit the guide topic and you should still compare prices, rules, and official information yourself.

### Full Disclosure

Use this on privacy/disclaimer or a future affiliate policy page:

> Taiwan Starter Guide may earn a commission when readers buy through selected partner links. This helps support the site. Partner links do not change the price you pay and do not guarantee that a product, service, or provider is the best choice for your situation. We try to keep recommendations practical and relevant, but you should always compare current prices, terms, cancellation rules, eligibility, and official information before buying. For tax, immigration, labor, health insurance, banking, remittance, or financial topics, official government and provider rules always prevail.

### Button Label Rules

Avoid aggressive copy:

- Do not use: "Best deal", "Guaranteed", "Official recommendation", "Must buy", "Cheapest".
- Prefer: "Compare options", "Check current price", "See provider details", "Check availability".

## Data Needed Before Adding Affiliate Blocks

Do not add affiliate blocks just because a program exists. Add them only after the page shows demand.

Minimum signals before testing:

- At least 30 days of public traffic data.
- At least 1,000 monthly visitors site-wide.
- At least 3 pages bringing consistent traffic from search, social, or direct sharing.
- At least 100 monthly visits to the page where the affiliate block would appear.
- At least 30 neutral outbound clicks from that page to useful non-affiliate resources.
- At least 5 pieces of real user feedback, search data, or behavior showing the topic matters.
- No major unresolved official-source warnings on that page.
- No broken links, placeholder text, or missing disclosure language.

Better signals:

- 2% to 8% outbound click rate on useful links.
- Visitors spend enough time to read the page instead of bouncing immediately.
- Users search for the topic inside the guide library.
- Friends or beta testers say the page helped them take a real next step.
- The page has a realistic example and clear "what to do next" section.

## Recommended Rollout

### Phase 0: Trust First

Timeline: Now to day 30.

Actions:

- Keep publishing useful English guide pages.
- Improve guide cards with specific Taiwan examples.
- Track clicks to official and neutral resources.
- Collect beta feedback through Netlify Forms.
- Do not add affiliate links.
- Do not add AdSense.

Success looks like:

- People can use the site without asking what to click.
- Friends can share the site with newcomers.
- The strongest guide topics become obvious from traffic and feedback.

### Phase 1: Soft Recommendation Testing

Timeline: Day 31 to day 90.

Actions:

- Add non-affiliate "useful options to compare" sections first.
- Measure clicks before replacing any with partner links.
- Keep official links and practical steps above any commercial option.
- Add disclosure language even before the first partner link, if the section is designed for future monetization.

Best first test pages:

- SIM / eSIM.
- Airport transport.
- Moving checklist.
- Short-term accommodation before renting.

Success looks like:

- Users click helpful outbound resources naturally.
- Affiliate-style sections do not reduce trust or feedback quality.
- The page still reads like a guide, not a product list.

### Phase 2: Limited Affiliate Test

Timeline: After 90 days, only if traffic and trust signals are healthy.

Actions:

- Test one category at a time.
- Start with one or two pages, not the whole site.
- Add clear disclosure next to each block.
- Keep affiliate links visually secondary to the guide content.
- Track clicks and feedback.
- Remove or revise any block that feels pushy or gets low-quality clicks.

Best first categories:

- SIM / eSIM.
- Airport pickup or transport support.
- Short-term accommodation.
- Daily arrival checklist products.

Do not test:

- Remittance.
- Credit cards.
- Loans.
- Investments.
- Sensitive official-rule pages.

## How Affiliate Blocks Should Look

Affiliate blocks should be small, practical, and optional.

Good structure:

1. User problem.
2. What to compare.
3. Neutral checklist.
4. Optional provider links.
5. Disclosure.

Example structure for a SIM page:

> If you are landing at Taoyuan Airport and need data immediately, compare tourist SIM, eSIM, and prepaid SIM options. Check validity days, data limit, hotspot support, whether a Taiwan phone number is included, and whether your phone supports eSIM.

Then add optional links only after the comparison guidance.

## What Not To Do

Do not:

- Put affiliate cards above the short answer.
- Hide official sources below commercial links.
- Recommend one provider without explaining tradeoffs.
- Add fake ratings.
- Say "best" without data.
- Add too many buttons.
- Use countdowns, fake urgency, or discount pressure.
- Put commercial links on tax, ARC, NHI, work permit, labor, banking, remittance, or emergency pages.

## Manual Review Before Any Affiliate Launch

Before adding the first partner link, confirm:

- The affiliate program accepts Taiwan traffic and English-language content.
- The program allows informational guide websites.
- The commission rules and tracking rules are clear.
- The page has a disclosure near the link.
- The privacy policy explains tracking if cookies, pixels, or analytics are added.
- The recommendation does not conflict with official guidance.
- The page is helpful even if all affiliate links are removed.

## Best First Monetization Experiment

The safest first experiment is:

1. Improve the SIM / eSIM page.
2. Add a neutral comparison table with no affiliate links.
3. Track outbound clicks to official telecom or neutral provider pages.
4. After 30 to 60 days of data, test one clearly disclosed partner link if user behavior supports it.

This fits the site because a phone connection is a real first-week need, the category is lower risk than finance or labor, and the page can remain useful without pushing a purchase.
