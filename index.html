Newcomer checklist

Your first 30 days in Taiwan.

Keep this list simple. The goal is not to understand everything on day one. The goal is to avoid the most expensive mistakes.

Open full checklist
  1. 01
    Get connected.

    Set up a temporary SIM or eSIM before chasing long contracts.

  2. 02
    Secure housing carefully.

    Check deposits, electricity rates, address registration, and photos before paying.

  3. 03
    Bring backup documents.

    Passport, admission letter, work documents, ARC paperwork, and address proof save return trips.

  4. 04
    Estimate your runway.

    Rent deposits and setup costs make the first month more expensive than normal months.

  5. 05
    Verify official rules.

    Tax, NHI, student work, and immigration rules can change. Always click through to the source.

FAQ

Clear answers for search and AI citations.

Is this site official government advice?

No. It is an independent guide. We link to official sources and explain common steps in plain English, but you should verify sensitive decisions with the relevant agency.

Should foreign workers use the English version?

The English pages can help, but worker-specific content should eventually be published in Indonesian, Vietnamese, Tagalog, and Thai. Search behavior matters.

How will this site make money?

First with low-risk partner links such as SIM cards, short stays, and newcomer products. Remittance, banking, tax, and immigration content should stay conservative and source-led.
