## Goal For This Week

Get real newcomer feedback before adding ads or affiliate links.

This week is not about revenue yet. It is about proving that real people find the site useful enough to click, read, search, and send feedback.

## Who To Ask First

Start with people who can give honest feedback:

- International students in Taiwan
- Foreign coworkers or classmates
- Taiwanese friends who help foreigners
- School international office contacts
- Expats who have lived in Taiwan for 1-3 years
- Newcomers who recently handled SIM, ARC, renting, bank, NHI, or transportation

## Questions To Ask Testers

Ask these after they try the site:

1. What page did you open first?
2. What information was actually useful?
3. What felt too shallow?
4. What question did you still have after reading?
5. Would you save or share this site with a newcomer?
6. Which topic should be improved first?
7. Did the site feel trustworthy?

## Signals That The Site Has Income Potential

Good signs:

- People ask where to buy SIM / eSIM.
- People ask about airport arrival or transport cards.
- People ask about short-term stay before renting.
- People ask for a checklist before moving to Taiwan.
- People save or share one of the tools.
- People say a guide helped them avoid a mistake.

Weak signs:

- People open only the homepage and leave.
- No one uses the tools.
- No one sends feedback.
- People say the content is too generic.
- People do not understand who the site is for.

## What To Improve First From Feedback

Prioritize pages that have both:

- real user questions
- low-risk monetization potential

Best first priorities:

1. SIM / eSIM
2. Transportation / EasyCard
3. Cost of living
4. Renting
5. Opening a bank account

Avoid monetization on:

- tax
- ARC
- NHI
- work permit
- labor rights
- banking
- remittance
- financial products

## Rule For Posting In Groups

Lead with usefulness, not promotion.

Good:

> I’m building a free beta guide and would love practical feedback from people who have moved to Taiwan.

Bad:

> Check out my new website and subscribe.
