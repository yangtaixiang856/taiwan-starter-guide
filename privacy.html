Privacy policy

Taiwan Starter Guide is a static information website. Newsletter and feedback forms are handled through Netlify Forms when the site is deployed on Netlify, with an email fallback for local or failed submissions.

We use Google Analytics to understand basic site usage, such as page views, guide searches, internal guide clicks, external link clicks, calculator use, and form submission events. We do not intentionally send form messages, email addresses, or other personal details to Google Analytics.

You can reduce analytics collection by using browser privacy settings, analytics blockers, or Do Not Track where supported. If ads, affiliate tracking, or additional analytics tools are added later, this policy should be updated before those tools are launched.
