Editorial standards

How we verify information

Taiwan Starter Guide is independent. The goal is to help newcomers understand what to ask, then point them to official sources for decisions that matter.

Short answer

For tax, immigration, labor, health insurance, and financial topics, official agencies are the source of truth. Our guides explain the workflow, examples, and common mistakes, but they do not replace official advice.

What counts as a primary source?

  • Ministry of Finance and eTax pages for tax topics.
  • National Immigration Agency pages for ARC, residence, and immigration topics.
  • National Health Insurance Administration pages for NHI topics.
  • Ministry of Labor or Workforce Development Agency pages for work permits and labor topics.
  • Regulator or official agency notices for financial-service topics.

How examples are written

Examples are realistic scenarios, not claims that every reader will have the same result. A student working part-time, a worker receiving monthly salary, or a newcomer renting a shared room may each need different official confirmation.

Update policy

Every sensitive guide should be reviewed before publishing and again whenever an official source changes. If a page mentions a deadline, eligibility rule, tax rate, permit route, or government process, the page should show a recent update date and link to the source.

What readers should do

Use the guide to understand the question, collect documents, and avoid common mistakes. Before filing tax, changing immigration status, starting work, relying on health insurance, or using financial services, confirm with the relevant agency or a qualified professional.
