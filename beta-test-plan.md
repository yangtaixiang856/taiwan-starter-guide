# Taiwan Starter Guide Beta Test Plan

Last updated: 2026-05-20

## Beta goal

Use this version with real people before adding more articles. The goal is to learn whether newcomers can find useful answers quickly, not to make the site perfect.

## Who to invite first

- 2 international students in Taiwan
- 1 expat or white-collar foreign worker
- 1 person who recently moved to Taiwan
- 1 person who has never seen the website before

## Link to send

Use the current Netlify beta URL while testing:

`https://heroic-speculoos-e83aec.netlify.app/`

If the site moves to a new Netlify URL or custom domain, replace this with the real production domain.

## Message you can send to friends

Hi, I am testing a new English website for foreigners living in Taiwan:

Taiwan Starter Guide

Could you spend 5 minutes trying it and tell me:

- What page you opened first
- What you searched for
- Whether the answer felt useful
- What felt confusing or missing
- Whether any link, button, language switch, or calculator broke

Please be honest. Short feedback is fine.

## Test tasks

Ask each tester to try 3-5 tasks:

- Find how to get a SIM or eSIM in Taiwan.
- Search for bank account information.
- Estimate monthly living costs.
- Find the 183-day tax rule.
- Find what an ARC is.
- Find NHI information.
- Switch language and return to English.
- Use the copy-link button on one guide.
- Open the contact page and check how to send feedback.

## Beta success signals

- At least 3 testers can find a useful page within one minute.
- At least 2 testers use search or a calculator without explanation.
- Testers can explain what the site is for after seeing the homepage.
- No tester gets stuck because of a broken button or language switch.
- Feedback points to 3-5 clear improvements instead of vague confusion.

## Do not optimize yet

- Do not add 50 more articles before feedback.
- Do not apply for AdSense yet.
- Do not add financial affiliate links yet.
- Do not translate everything with machine translation.
- Do not replace official-source reminders with confident but unverifiable claims.
